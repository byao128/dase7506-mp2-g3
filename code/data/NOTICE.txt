The upstream model is Apache-2.0 licensed. No model weights are included here.
This model attribution does not replace the original GSM8K dataset license.

The course bank is a filtered and partitioned derivative with four fixed
candidate traces per question. Counts and SHA256 hashes are in manifest.json.
Its selection accuracy is not the original GSM8K generation benchmark score.

Candidate traces are machine-generated and may contain erroneous reasoning,
even when their final answers match the references. No participant submission,
student identifier, teacher checkpoint, or private audit bank is included.
